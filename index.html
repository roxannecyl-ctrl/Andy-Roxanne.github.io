A LITTLE NOTE FOR YOU
Dear Andy

How has it already been two years? Somehow, it feels like we only just met, and at the same time, it’s hard to imagine my everyday life without you in it.

I’m so grateful for these two years with you—and, of course, a little grateful to myself for saying “hello” to you at Padre that night. You’d better be grateful for that too! Without my very important contribution, we might never have met. So really, you’re welcome.

These two years have made me so happy. Of course, we’ve had our arguments and little moments of friction, but thank you for always caring for me, being patient with me, and making room for me to be myself.

I know I’m not always the easiest person, and I don’t take your kindness or the way you look after me for granted.

When I think about what I love most about being with you, it’s often the simplest things: going for a walk, cooking together, or watching TV side by side.

And those quiet mornings when you’re still asleep and I’m next to you. Just hearing you breathe makes me feel safe. Those are the moments I want to keep forever—my little golden memory.

Thank you for all the little ways you care for me, including the ones I might forget to thank you for in the moment. Thank you for listening, for putting up with my moods, and for making me feel loved in our everyday life.

I don’t expect us to get everything right all the time. I just hope we keep listening to each other, laughing together, and finding our way back to each other when things get a little difficult.

Happy two years, my love. I love you.
Yours, Roxanne ❤︎
